# Owner research

Owner work, not agent tasks. Results feed PROJECT D-55, D-58, D-69, D-80–D-82 and Q-12.

Interview questions for 5–10 freelancers:

1. Your last three cross-border client payments: who chose the method, which currency and route, which arrived?
2. USDC or USDT received for client work in the last 90 days: which network, exchange or wallet, and your net
   amount after conversion and bank fees?
3. The last time an exchange or accountant asked where a payment came from, what did you show?
4. When did a client refuse or struggle to pay in crypto, and what did you use instead?
5. What do you pay today for invoicing, stablecoin receipt and cash-out? Would you pick free with a visible 1% fee,
   a monthly plan, or your current method?
6. Your last late payment: what terms, how many days late, and what did you do?
7. Do you take a deposit? What share? Has a client refused it, and did you lose the client?
8. Which steps did a client's finance team ask for: vendor form, W-9 or W-8BEN, PO number, portal, approval? Did any
   delay payment?
9. Who reminds clients today, you or a tool? Would you let a tool email your client's finance contact on its own?
10. How do you record a stablecoin payment's USD or EUR value, and what did your accountant, bank or exchange ask
    for?
11. Here is the job flow (quote → deposit → milestones → final). Would you use it with your next client? What would
    stop you?

Questions for 2–3 people who approve or pay vendor invoices (a client's finance side):

1. What must a contractor invoice show before you can pay it?
2. Could your team pay an invoice in USDC? Who would have to approve that, and what would they need to see?
3. Would an automatic reminder from a contractor's invoicing tool help you, or annoy you?
4. Would your team pay a contractor's invoice by company card, and what happens when someone disputes it?
