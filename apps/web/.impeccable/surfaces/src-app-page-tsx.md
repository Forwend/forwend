# Landing surface brief

Scope: `/` landing (Persuade), plus the private `/kit/directions` comparison and `/kit` (Operate) that share its world.
Audience: freelancers and merchants paid in stablecoins, pre-launch visitors. Action: join the waitlist.
Proof available: none commercial. Demonstration data only, labeled as sample.
Constraints: no logos, funding badges, eyebrow labels, or claims of being live, regulated or insured. Mood art is code-drawn SVG until the Codex-generated images (F002, docs/web/images.md) are wired into the landing.
Owner choice pending: the owner reviews `/kit/directions` and may switch from the default below.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The path forward is the payment itself. The river that runs from the visitor toward the horizon is the progress line of an invoice: detected, confirming, paid, settled. Refuses the category default of a dark crypto hero with glowing coins and a hero metric.

OWN-WORLD: Dawn Path. Cool dawn sky to pale mist, meadow greens, river blue, one deep green-black ink for type and primary pills; no cream. Display serif (Libre Caslon Display) over a quiet grotesk (Hanken Grotesk); every amount and address in Geist Mono with tabular figures. Floating white pill nav, ink pill buttons, translucent panels only where they sit on landscape. Product showcases drop into dusk-ink framed sections (raise from Dusk Ledger). Halftone dot fields as a secondary texture (raise from Halftone Horizon). Raise, split-flap concourse: a status changes in place, row geometry never moves. Raise, ikebana ma: asymmetric, charged emptiness in the sky, never filled with badges.

STORY: Visitor sees a calm landscape and an invoice moving along the river to "Settled", understands where money is at each step, reads how exceptions are handled, joins the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inset rounded frame filled by the landscape. Pill nav centered at top, "Join waitlist" ink pill at its right end. Serif headline centered in the upper third of open sky, one-line subhead, email capture beneath. Lower third: a sample invoice panel on the river, its timeline advancing to Settled while the river path draws forward.

FORM: Brief-pinned Dawn Path with Dusk Ledger and Halftone Horizon raises; roll assigned grounded index 4, superseded by the pin. Seed key a7c7d75a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
